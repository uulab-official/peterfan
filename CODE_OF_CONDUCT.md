# Code of conduct

PeterFan welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience. In issues, pull requests and other project spaces:

- Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique the work and explain the
  evidence behind disagreements.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, insult, or publish another person's
  private information.
- Respect boundaries and requests to stop an interaction. Avoid unsolicited
  sexual content, spam and disruptive promotion.
- Keep hardware reports factual. Do not pressure others to disable safeguards
  or share private diagnostics.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[uulab.official@gmail.com](mailto:uulab.official@gmail.com), including relevant
links and context with personal information removed. Reports are reviewed on a
best-effort basis. Maintainers may request corrections, remove inappropriate
content, close a discussion or restrict participation depending on severity and
repeated behavior. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so does
not expose private information.

This policy applies to project spaces and project-related interactions; it does
not authorize harassment or investigation of contributors outside the project.
